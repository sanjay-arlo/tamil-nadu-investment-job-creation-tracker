# Tamil Nadu Investment & Job-Creation Realisation Tracker

> Business Analyst portfolio case study tracking announced investment, realised investment and promised-versus-verified jobs by sector and district.

## 🚀 Live Dashboard

**[Open the interactive dashboard](https://sanjay-arlo.github.io/tamil-nadu-investment-job-creation-tracker/)**

## Business question

**How much announced investment and how many promised jobs have actually materialised, by sector and district?**

## Dashboard

Compare announced and realised investment, jobs promised versus verified, sector concentration, district spread and time-to-realisation.

## KPI logic

**Conversion %** = verified realised investment / announced MoU value × 100.

**Jobs gap** = jobs promised − jobs verified.

## Dataset

Synthetic/illustrative MoU records are used for portfolio demonstration. They are not official Guidance Tamil Nadu investment tracking records.

## Production upgrade

Use verified MoU milestones, operating-status evidence, employment verification, district master data and dated source lineage. Add ageing alerts and owner-based follow-up workflows.

## Limitation

Portfolio case study only; it is not an investment audit.
