# BA Handoff — Tamil Nadu Investment & Job-Creation Tracker

## Objective
Track announced investment against realised investment and promised versus verified jobs by sector and district.

## Stakeholders
State investment leadership; district administration; industry departments; company/project owners; employment-verification teams; finance/audit.

## Core requirements
- Compare announced and realised investment.
- Track jobs promised vs verified.
- Analyse sector and district concentration.
- Flag ageing/unrealised projects.
- Preserve source lineage and milestone dates.

## Decision rule
Prioritise high-value projects with low realisation or large jobs gaps and assign an owner for follow-up.

## Acceptance criteria
Conversion and jobs-gap KPIs reconcile; sector/district filters work; ageing logic is explainable; synthetic-data disclaimer remains visible.

## Production controls
Verified MoU milestones, operating evidence, employment verification, district master, ageing alerts, source lineage and audit trail.
